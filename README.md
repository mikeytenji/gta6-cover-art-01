## PANEL HEAT — a GTA 6-style cover-art studio

PANEL HEAT turns a photo and a caption into a GTA 6-style poster cover, entirely in the browser. It is my entry to the [make.fun "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

How it works:

- **Photos** — drop in your own pictures, or start from the two built-in Leonida-flavoured samples (the pier and the boulevard).
- **Layout** — arrange the shots in comic-style panels: Duo, Trio or Quad.
- **Grade** — give the cover its look with a Sunset, Neon or Noir colour grade.
- **Captions** — set the cover's title lines, pick a wide, square or tall poster size, and export the finished cover as a PNG.

Everything runs client-side with no build step and no external assets: open `app/index.html` through the bundled `serve.ts` server and the studio is ready. The `screenshots/` folder shows the editor and three finished covers (sunset quad, neon trio, tall duo), and `main.ts` packages the app and its screenshots into the entry dossier.
